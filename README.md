# Online Food Ordering System

## 1. System Request

* **Project Name:** Online Food Ordering System
* **Project Sponsor:** Restaurant Management / Owner

### Business Need
The restaurant currently faces high call volumes and manual order errors during peak hours, leading to delayed deliveries and lost customers. An online system is needed to streamline order placement, improve efficiency, and enhance customer satisfaction.

### Business Requirements
1. **User Authentication:** Customer registration and login functionality.
2. **Menu Browsing:** Interactive digital menu with categories, prices, and search.
3. **Cart Management:** Ability to add, modify, and review ordered items.
4. **Order & Payment Processing:** Support for online payment methods and cash on delivery.
5. **Order Tracking:** Real-time updates on order preparation and delivery status.
6. **Admin Dashboard:** Management interface for updating menu items, prices, and viewing incoming orders.

### Business Value
* Expected 25% increase in total sales by reaching online customers.
* Reduction in order processing errors by up to 90%.
* Faster customer service and improved brand loyalty.

### Special Issues / Constraints
* Responsive web design suitable for both mobile and desktop screens.
* Secure handling of user data and payment transactions.

## 2. Feasibility Study

### Technical Feasibility
* **Familiarity with Technology:** The development team possesses standard web development skills (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and database integration).
* **Project Size:** The scope is moderate, manageable, and fits within the standard course project requirements.

### Economic Feasibility
* **Costs:** Development costs (tools and software) and operational costs (domain registration and cloud hosting).
* **Benefits:** Tangible benefits include increased revenue and lower manual labor costs. Intangible benefits include better customer experience and brand identity.

### Organizational Feasibility
* **User Acceptance:** High acceptance is expected from both customers and restaurant staff due to the system's ease of use and time-saving features.
